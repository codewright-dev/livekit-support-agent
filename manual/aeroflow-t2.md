# AeroFlow T2 Smart Thermostat: Support Manual

> Fictional product used for the demo.

## Package contents
The box contains the T2 thermostat, a wall plate, four screws and anchors, a C-wire adapter, wire labels and a quick-start card.

## Compatibility
The T2 works with most 24 V heating and cooling systems: gas, oil and electric furnaces, heat pumps (with or without auxiliary heat) and central air conditioning. It does not support high-voltage (120 V or 240 V) baseboard or line-voltage heaters. A common wire (C-wire) is required; if your system has no C-wire, use the included C-wire adapter at the furnace control board.

## Installation
Turn off power to the heating and cooling system at the breaker before removing the old thermostat. Take a photo of the existing wiring, label each wire with the included labels, then remove the old unit. Mount the wall plate with the screws provided, connect the labelled wires to the matching terminals, attach the T2 to the plate and restore power. Installation takes about 30 minutes.

## Wi-Fi setup
The T2 connects to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks only; 5 GHz-only networks are not supported. Open the AeroFlow app, tap Add Device and follow the prompts. Keep your phone within 10 metres of the thermostat during setup. If your router broadcasts one name for both bands, temporarily enable a separate 2.4 GHz network name. WPA3-only networks must be switched to WPA2/WPA3 mixed mode.

## Status light
- Solid green: connected and working normally.
- Blinking blue: in Wi-Fi setup mode, waiting for the app.
- Blinking amber: no Wi-Fi connection; the thermostat keeps running its last schedule offline.
- Solid red: hardware or wiring fault; see the error codes section.

## Error codes
- E1, no power from the C-wire: check the C-wire connection or install the C-wire adapter.
- E2, temperature sensor fault: restart the thermostat; if E2 returns, contact support for a replacement.
- E3, heating system not responding: check the furnace switch and breaker, then confirm the W wire is seated in the W terminal.
- E4, cooling system not responding: check the outdoor unit breaker and the Y wire connection.
- E5, firmware update failed: keep the thermostat on Wi-Fi; it retries automatically within 24 hours.

## Schedules and eco mode
Create schedules in the app under Schedule. Each day supports up to six temperature changes. Eco mode widens the temperature range when nobody is home, using your phone's location (geofencing) to detect when the household leaves and returns. Typical savings are 10 to 15 percent on heating and cooling.

## Restart and factory reset
To restart, press and hold the dial for 10 seconds until the screen turns off, then release. To factory reset, open Settings on the thermostat, choose Reset, then Factory Reset; this erases schedules and Wi-Fi settings and removes the device from the app.

## Battery backup
The T2 has a small internal battery that keeps the clock and settings during power cuts of up to 4 hours. It does not run the heating or cooling system without power.

## Warranty and returns
The T2 has a 2-year limited warranty from the date of purchase, covering manufacturing defects. Proof of purchase and the serial number (on the back of the unit and on the box) are required. Returns for a refund are accepted within 30 days of purchase if the product is undamaged. Professional installation is not required to keep the warranty.
